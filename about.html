My story

Learning by building real projects.

I’m Caleb Kritzar, an incoming University of Toledo student beginning exploratory studies in Fall 2026. I plan to move into Information Technology as I continue discovering which areas best match my strengths and interests.

I learn best by creating and improving real projects. My current work includes maintaining BusArmyDude.org and developing Shareline as separate iOS and Android prototypes.

These projects allow me to practice web development, native interface design, accessibility, responsive layouts, testing, and continuous improvement.

I care about readable interfaces, consistent behavior, honest project descriptions, and technology that respects different user needs.
