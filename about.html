My story

I’m Caleb Kritzar, an incoming University of Toledo student beginning exploratory studies in Fall 2026. I plan to move into Information Technology as I continue learning which areas of technology best match my strengths and interests.

I learn by building. My current work includes maintaining BusArmyDude.org and developing Shareline as an iOS and Android prototype. These projects give me opportunities to practice web development, interface design, accessibility, and platform-specific thinking.

I care about clear organization, readable interfaces, consistent behavior, and honest presentation. I would rather show steady progress and explain what is still being developed than present unfinished work as complete.
